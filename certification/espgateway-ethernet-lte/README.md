## Completion criteria

1. Intertek accepts the identified sample, operating instructions and test configuration.
2. Applicable emissions testing is completed successfully and its report identifies the covered product/configuration.
3. Final label and FCC manual statements match the report and any applicable accessory restrictions.
4. An eligible US responsible party is established and the compliance information accompanies the product.
5. Design, production-conformity procedures, test records and compliance information are retained under 47 CFR 2.938; production changes are reviewed for continued coverage.

SDoC does not issue a new FCC ID. Radio-module integration remains a separate FCC boundary question; this pack does not claim SDoC testing establishes transmitter authorization.

Sources: [2.906, 2.909, 2.938, 2.1074 and 2.1077](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title47-vol1/xhtml/CFR-2025-title47-vol1-part2-subpartJ.html).
